Partner Onboarding

To partner your restaurant with Swiggy, visit partner.swiggy.com and register your restaurant. You'll need to provide basic details about your restaurant including FSSAI license, PAN card, and bank account information. Our team will reach out to you within 24-48 hours after submission.

You'll need: FSSAI License, PAN Card, GST Certificate, Bank Account details, and a valid mobile number. For a complete list, visit the partner portal.

To opt-out from Google Reserve, please log in to your Swiggy Partner portal and navigate to Settings > Integrations > Google Reserve, then disable it from there.

Once all documents are verified and approved, your restaurant typically goes live within 5-7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email and SMS once your restaurant is live.

Swiggy charges a one-time onboarding fee which covers the cost of menu photography, cataloguing, and training. This is paid after your restaurant is approved. You do not need to pay during registration.

You can contact your dedicated onboarding manager assigned to you, or reach out through the Partner Support portal at 1800-208-9999 (Mon-Sat, 10 AM – 6 PM).

FAQs

Once your order is placed, you can track it in real-time from the Order Tracking page. You'll receive SMS and push notifications at each stage: Order confirmed → Preparing → Out for delivery → Delivered.

If your order is significantly delayed, you can contact our support through the Help section in the app. We will investigate and provide an update as quickly as possible.

On the checkout page, tap "Add a promo code" and enter your code. Valid codes will be applied automatically and the discount reflected in your bill.

Address changes after placing an order are not possible in most cases. Please ensure you select the correct delivery address before placing your order.

Swiggy accepts UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Swiggy Money, and Cash on Delivery (COD) where available. You can also use Swiggy One discounts if you have a subscription.

Instamart Onboarding

Visit the Swiggy Instamart partner portal and submit your grocery/retail store details. Instamart focuses on hyperlocal delivery for grocery stores, pharmacies, and daily essentials.

Grocery stores, supermarkets, pharmacies, pet stores, home essentials stores, baby care stores, and electronics stores can all partner with Swiggy Instamart.

IRCTC FAQ

Swiggy has partnered with IRCTC to deliver food directly to your train seat. Go to the Swiggy app, select "Food on Train", enter your PNR number and seat details, and choose from restaurants at your upcoming station.

Train food orders can be cancelled up to 60 minutes before the scheduled delivery station stop. After that, cancellations may not be possible.
